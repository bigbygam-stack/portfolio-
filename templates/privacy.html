Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This is the personal portfolio website of Gamir Bigby. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and who else handles it.

1. What I collect

Contact form. If you send a message, I collect the name, email address and message you type, plus the date and time you sent it.

Weather page. The city you search for is sent to Open-Meteo, a public weather service, to look up the weather. My site does not save the cities you search for.

Hosting logs. My hosting provider, Vercel, may automatically record standard technical information about visits, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. I don't use this to track individual visitors.

2. Cookies

The site uses a small, temporary cookie to show a confirmation or error notice after you submit a form. It is not used for tracking. I don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How I use your information

I use contact form details only to read and reply to your message. I don't sell your information or use it for marketing.

4. Who else handles it

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Neon hosts the database where contact messages are stored.
  • Open-Meteo receives the city names searched on the weather page.

These services have their own privacy policies. I may also share information if the law requires it.

5. How long I keep it

I keep messages for as long as I need them to respond, and I may delete them afterwards. You can ask me to delete yours at any time.

6. Your choices

To ask what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, email me at YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. I will respond as soon as I reasonably can.

7. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, including using HTTPS and keeping my database private. No website can guarantee perfect security, so please don't send sensitive information through the contact form.

8. Children

This site isn't aimed at children, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If I change how the site works, I will update this page and the date at the top.
